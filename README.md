**Just Another Video Player** (JAVP) is the free, open-source way to watch all your movies, TV shows, live channels, and personal media — no matter where you keep them. If you have an IPTV subscription, a Jellyfin/Emby/Plex server, or a folder full of videos, JAVP brings everything together in one clean, easy-to-use app.

It works on **Android phones, Android TV (like Chromecast with Google TV or Nvidia Shield), Windows computers, Linux, and Macs.** That means your media library follows you everywhere, and the interface looks great on every screen.

## ✨ What Makes JAVP Special?

- **Bring Your Own Media** – JAVP doesn’t lock you into a service. Connect your own IPTV playlists (M3U or Xtream codes), your home media server (Jellyfin, Emby, or Plex), or just point it at a folder of video files.
- **One Login, All Libraries** – If you already use Jellyfin or Emby, JAVP lets you browse and play all your existing libraries with a familiar, modern interface.
- **No Ads, No Tracking, No Cost** – This is a genuine free and open-source project (GPLv3 license). You can even view the source code and contribute if you like.
- **Cross-Platform Magic** – Start watching on your phone, continue on your TV, and finish on your laptop. JAVP syncs your progress and remembers where you left off.
- **Clean & Simple Design** – No confusing menus. Big buttons, clear labels, and a layout that works with a remote control, a touchscreen, or a mouse.
- **Privacy First** – Your watch history and personal media are stored only on your own devices. JAVP doesn’t phone home or collect your data.

### 3️⃣ First Launch

When you open JAVP for the first time, you’ll see a welcome screen. Don’t worry — you don’t need to set up anything right away. You’ll be able to add your media sources in just a minute.

JAVP will ask for permission to access your local files. Click **Allow** — this lets it play videos stored on your computer.

### 4️⃣ Add Your Media (Pick One — Or All!)

JAVP is all about flexibility. Here’s how to connect your favorite sources:

- **For IPTV (M3U or Xtream Codes):** If you have an IPTV subscription, look for a **"+"** button or an "Add Source" menu. Choose **"IPTV"** or **"M3U"**. Paste your M3U URL or Xtream username/password/URL combo. Name it (e.g., "My Live TV") and press **Save**. JAVP will load your channel list instantly.
- **For Jellyfin, Emby, or Plex:** In the same "Add Source" menu, select **Jellyfin**, **Emby**, or **Plex**. Enter the server address (for example, `http://192.168.1.10:8096` for Jellyfin) and your username/password. JAVP will connect and show all your movies, shows, and home videos.
- **For Local Files / Folders:** Click the **"Browse"** or **"Library"** tab. Use the file picker to navigate to a folder where you keep videos (e.g., `C:\Users\YourName\Videos`). JAVP will scan that folder and add all compatible video files to your library.

### 5️⃣ Start Watching!

That’s it! Browse around. You’ll see posters for your movies, episode lists for TV shows, and a grid of live channels under IPTV. Click anything to play it.

Use the on-screen playback controls to pause, rewind, fast-forward, or switch subtitles. JAVP remembers what you were watching, so if you close the app and reopen it, you’ll get a "Resume from where you left off?" prompt.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is JAVP really free?
Yes. 100% free, no ads, no premium tier. It’s developed by volunteers and funded by community donations. You can even download the source code and build it yourself if you’re curious.

### Do I need a server to use JAVP?
No. You can use JAVP with just a folder of videos on your hard drive. But it also works beautifully with Jellyfin/Emby/Plex servers and IPTV subscriptions.

### What file formats are supported?
JAVP uses modern video codecs. Most common formats work: MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, and more. For audio, AAC, MP3, FLAC, and AC3 are typical. For subtitles, it handles SRT, VTT, and embedded subtitle tracks.

### Can I watch live TV?
Yes, if you have an IPTV subscription. JAVP supports M3U playlists and Xtream Codes, giving you live channels, catch-up TV, and an electronic program guide (EPG) if your provider offers one.

### Does JAVP sync across devices?
Absolutely. Because you’re logging into your own Jellyfin/Emby/Plex server, your watch progress and playlists are stored on that server. JAVP just reads from it. So you can pause on the TV and resume on your phone in seconds.

### Will JAVP slow down my computer?
No. JAVP is lightweight. It uses hardware acceleration for video decoding, meaning it uses your computer’s graphics processor (GPU) to play video smoothly without maxing out your CPU. The background interface is minimal and won’t drain resources.

### Is my data safe?
Your login credentials are stored only on your device, sent directly to your chosen server with standard encryption. JAVP has no central cloud — there’s nothing to hack, and no one else can see your media.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting Common Issues

| Problem | Quick Fix |
|---------|-----------|
| **Video plays but no sound** | Check the audio track selector (the speaker icon) and make sure it's on "Default" or your preferred language. If using an IPTV channel, switch to a different audio track. |
| **Can't connect to my Jellyfin server** | Make sure your server is running. Type the server address into your web browser to ensure it's reachable. Double-check that you used `http://` or `https://` correctly. |
| **IPTV channel keeps buffering** | Try lowering the stream quality in playback settings (e.g., from 1080p to 720p). Your internet connection or the provider may be limited. |
| **JAVP won't open on Windows** | Right-click JAVP.exe → Properties → Compatibility → Run as Administrator. Also, ensure you have the latest graphics drivers installed. |
| **Library shows duplicate movies** | Go to Settings → Library and enable "Skip duplicate titles based on year." |
| **Subtitles aren't showing** | During playback, click the "CC" icon and select your subtitle track. If using external .srt files, make sure they have the exact same name as the video file (e.g., `Movie.mp4` and `Movie.srt` in the same folder). |

## 💡 Pro Tips for Power Users

- Use the **"Search"** bar at the top to quickly find a movie or episode across all your sources.
- In **Settings → Playback**, you can set a default bitrate or enable "Always keep subtitles on."
- If you love keyboard use, press **F** for fullscreen, **Space** to play/pause, and **M** to mute on desktop.
- On Android TV, press the **Guide** button (if your remote has one) to quickly open the IPTV channel guide.
- You can rename your IPTV channels directly in JAVP. Just long-press a channel and choose "Edit Channel" — perfect for fixing provider typos.
